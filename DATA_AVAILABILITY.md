# Restricted research data

This repository is intended for private, authorized research access only. The owner requested preservation of the original research inputs. Data are therefore deliberately tracked, rather than silently removed by `.gitignore`.

The records are **not anonymized**. They contain work-order, engine and chassis identifiers, dates, locations, mileage and maintenance costs. Free text may contain additional identifying information. Do not make the repository public, publish a fork or release archive, or provide access to additional recipients without appropriate authorization and a dedicated disclosure review.

The source-code MIT license does not grant rights to use, publish, redistribute or sublicense these records. Data rights remain with their respective owners.

The seed annotations are preannotations pending expert review. The unseen reference labels are machine-generated silver labels. Historical directory names containing `gold` do not establish expert adjudication. These distinctions must be retained in documentation and evaluation reports.

The main raw spreadsheet is not bundled. The original document-level JSONL inputs, annotation splits, reference labels, pending review sheet and terminology knowledge base are preserved byte-for-byte. See `docs/data_manifest.json` for provenance and checksums.
